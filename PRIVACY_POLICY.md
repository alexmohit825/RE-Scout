# Privacy Policy for RE Scout Pro

**Last Updated:** August 30, 2026  
**Developer:** Dr. A. Alex Mohit  
**Application:** RE Scout Pro (iOS)

---

## 1. Overview
RE Scout Pro ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled when using RE Scout Pro on iOS and iPadOS devices.

RE Scout Pro is designed as a **local-first commercial & residential real estate financial underwriter**. We do not track users, collect personal financial portfolios, sell user data, or display third-party advertisements.

---

## 2. Information We Access & Process

### A. Financial Underwriting & Property Data
* **Usage:** Property metrics, acquisition prices, net operating income (NOI), cap rates, rental rolls, expense ratios, and mortgage calculations entered into the App are processed locally on your device.
* **Storage:** All financial models and pro forma calculations are saved exclusively on your local device sandbox. We do not store or transmit your private deal analysis to external servers.

### B. Location Data (GPS)
* **Usage:** If granted permission (`NSLocationWhenInUseUsageDescription`), the App accesses your location solely to assist in identifying local market submarkets and logging property inspection addresses.
* **Storage:** Location data is stored locally and is never shared, aggregated, or sold.

### C. AI Market Research & Valuation Assistance (Gemini API)
* **Usage:** When you request an AI market assessment or property synthesis, property parameters (e.g. zip code, asset class, square footage) are transmitted securely via HTTPS to Google's Gemini API to generate real-time market comps and underwriting summaries.
* **Data Handling:** Queries do not contain personally identifiable information (PII) or financial account credentials.

---

## 3. Data Sharing & Third Parties
* **Zero Tracking:** We do not track user activity across other apps or websites.
* **No Advertising Frameworks:** The App contains zero ad networks, third-party analytics trackers, or user profiling SDKs.
* **No Data Monetization:** We never sell, rent, or trade your deal pipeline or personal information.

---

## 4. User Rights & Data Retention
You have complete ownership and control over all property underwriting data stored within RE Scout Pro. You can modify or delete saved properties at any time directly in the App.
